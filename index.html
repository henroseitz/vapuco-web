ABOUT VAPUCO

Your Reliable Industrial Partner

VAPUCO s.r.o. is a Slovak company based in Šaľa, specializing in the supply of industrial valves, pumps and compressors for demanding industrial applications.

As the exclusive representative of Baltic Valves and Valvosider for Slovakia, we provide reliable industrial solutions supported by leading European manufacturers. We also offer selected products from internationally recognized brands, including Bonney Forge, available from stock or on request.

Founded as a family business, VAPUCO combines personal commitment, technical expertise and responsive customer service. Our goal is to build long-term partnerships by delivering reliable products, short lead times and professional support.

As a growing family business, we are continuously expanding our portfolio to provide complete industrial solutions in valves, pumps and compressors while maintaining the personal approach and reliability our customers expect.
